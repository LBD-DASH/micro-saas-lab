This describes a planned tool. **It is in development and not yet available.** Details may change based on what people tell us they need.

## The workflow

1. **Give it the enquiry.** Paste a customer message, a phone note or a short job description.
2. **Add your own details once.** Your usual services, units and rates. The tool would use what you give it and would not invent your prices.
3. **Get an itemised draft.** Line items, quantities, a short scope note and any assumptions it had to make, clearly flagged.
4. **Review and edit.** Every line is yours to change. Anything the tool was unsure about is highlighted for you to confirm.
5. **Send it yourself.** The tool would not email or message your customer for you.

## What it would not do

- Send quotes on its own.
- Guess your rates when it does not have them.
- Take payment.

## Why a waitlist first

We want to confirm that people actually want this before building it. If you do, [join the waitlist](../#waitlist).
