## Right now

The waitlist form is not switched on, so this site collects no personal information through it. We run no analytics and set no tracking cookies of our own.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visits as part of running its service; see [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## When the waitlist opens

We would ask for your email and, optionally, your name, business type and how you write quotes today. We would use these only to tell you about this tool and to decide what to build. We would not sell them. You could ask us to delete your details at any time. This page will be updated with the contact route and the service that stores the data before the form goes live.
