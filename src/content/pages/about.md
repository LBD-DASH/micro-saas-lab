Micro SaaS Lab is a small validation project run by Kevin Britz. The idea is simple: describe a small tool honestly, see whether people want it, and only then decide whether to build it.

## What we are testing now

One tool: drafting an itemised quote from a job description or enquiry for small service businesses. It is in development and not yet available.

## What we will not do

- Invent statistics, testimonials, users or results.
- Show prices before they exist.
- Take payments or sell anything on this site.
